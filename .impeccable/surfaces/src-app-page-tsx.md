# Surface brief — home / resume

- **Scope:** the single-page resume at `/`, all sections and the contact close.
- **Visitor mode:** Experience — the work and the record lead; the page must still do a Persuade job (convince a recruiter to make contact).
- **Audience:** Thai recruiters and hiring managers screening internship or junior developer roles, often on a phone between other tasks; secondary: potential freelance clients.
- **Job:** understand who Worawat is, see evidence of shipped software, and contact him for an interview or project.
- **Chosen direction:** Service Logbook — a clothbound maintenance log on a dark workbench, with manila index tabs, grid paper, dated service entries, a stapled portrait, and one red status stamp.
- **Memorable moment:** the project records are real work orders — the Tee Ob POS entry carries a working test-login slip and a live status stamp — and the right-edge tabs work as the page's index.
- **Constraints:** Thai copy, existing content, navy/cyan brand palette, excellent mobile; dark-mode toggle is not required. No invented employers, metrics, or testimonials.
